Format reference and examples

Guestbooks supports the date-only subset of strftime listed below, not Go date layouts or JavaScript library patterns. Other text, including quotes and backslashes, is literal. Write %% for a percent sign.

TokenMeaning
%Y, %yYear: four digits or last two digits
%mMonth, 01-12
%d, %eDay of month, padded to two characters with a zero or a space
%b, %BShort or full month name in the visitor's locale
%a, %AShort or full weekday name in the visitor's locale
%jDay of year, 001-366
%G, %gISO week-numbering year: four digits or last two digits (can differ from %Y near New Year)
%VISO week number, 01-53
%u, %wWeekday number: Monday=1 through Sunday=7, or Sunday=0 through Saturday=6
%%A literal percent sign

Examples: %d/%m/%Y, %b %d, %Y, compact ISO %Y%m%d, ISO week date %G-W%V-%u, ISO ordinal date %Y-%j. Dates use the Gregorian calendar. Names are localized using Intl.DateTimeFormat (MDN). Only the tokens listed here are supported; flags and time-of-day tokens are not.
